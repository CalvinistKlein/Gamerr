Platform Detection

How Romerr detects platforms from filenames

Romerr automatically detects platform information from ROM filenames using:

  • File extensions (.nes, .smc, .gba, .iso, etc.)
  • Filename patterns (platform names in parentheses or brackets)
  • Region detection from country codes in filenames
  • Title extraction by removing common tags and patterns

You can manually adjust detected information before importing.
